WHY WE BUILT SOSAiTi

Every Great Solution Begins With A Real Problem.

Residential communities deserve better tools to manage their daily operations. SOSAiTi was created to replace manual work, scattered communication and paperwork with one simple digital platform.

01

The Challenge

Managing a society often involves paperwork, manual maintenance records, missed notices and scattered communication across different platforms.

02

Our Solution

We built SOSAiTi to bring maintenance, complaints, notices and resident communication together in one secure and easy-to-use application.

03

The Result

A smarter, faster and more transparent way to manage residential communities while improving communication between residents and management committees.
